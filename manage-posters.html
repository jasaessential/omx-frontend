Poster Setup

Settings for Wall Posters → Customize. The customer picks a size, uploads a picture and fits it to the poster. Prices on the customize page come from here.

Paper GSM

Choose the paper weights you print on and mark one as the default (pre-selected for the customer). "Extra ₹" is added to the size price for that GSM (use 0 for none).

Sizes & Prices

Width × height in inches (portrait). The editor canvas uses this shape, so the picture the customer fits matches the print. A size with no price is hidden from customers.
