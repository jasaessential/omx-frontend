Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Your privacy matters to us
Overview
This Privacy Policy explains how JASA Essential ("Order My Xerox", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile app. We are committed to keeping your data safe and being transparent about how it is used.
1. Data We Collect
Data Type Examples Why
Account Info Name, email, phone number Account creation & authentication
Order Data Items ordered, delivery address, payment status Fulfilling your orders
Device Info Device model, OS version, push token App performance & notifications
Usage Data Pages visited, search queries, clicks Improving the app experience
Files (Xerox) Documents you upload for printing Processing your print order

We do not collect your precise GPS location.

2. How We Use Your Data
  • Process and fulfil your orders
  • Send order status updates and push notifications
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve app features and fix bugs
  • Send promotional offers (only with your consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations
3. Third-Party Services
We use the following trusted third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:
  • Google Firebase — Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Storage, Push Notifications
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Anonymous usage analytics
  • Google Fonts & Font Awesome CDN — UI icons and fonts
We do not sell your data to any third party for advertising purposes.
4. Data Sharing
We only share your data when:
  • It is necessary to fulfil your order (e.g., delivery staff see your delivery address)
  • Required by law or a valid government request
  • You have given explicit consent
We never sell your personal data.
5. Uploaded Files (Xerox Orders)
Documents you upload for xerox printing are stored securely on Firebase Cloud Storage. They are:
  • Accessed only by the fulfilment team to process your order
  • Automatically deleted after 30 days of order completion
  • Never shared with third parties or used for any other purpose
6. Data Retention
We retain your account data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.
7. Your Rights
You have the right to:
Access your data
Correct inaccuracies
Request deletion
Export your data
Opt out of marketing
Restrict processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Security
We use industry-standard security measures including:
  • Firebase Security Rules for all database and storage access
  • HTTPS / TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Firebase Authentication for secure sign-in
No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We strive to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.
9. Children's Privacy
Our service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.
10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes via an in-app notification. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
